# Product specification

Public MVP supports Polish and English CVs for six optimised technical role families: Frontend, Backend, Full-stack, Mobile, QA / Test Automation and DevOps / Platform, at Junior, Mid, Senior and Lead levels. Frontend has the deepest current semantic and live-model validation; support for the other five roles is backed by role-specific rubrics and synthetic cross-role cases, not an equivalent depth of production evidence. The form also offers `Other` as an explicitly experimental mode. It uses a general evidence-first rubric and infers occupational context only from the CV, so it is not presented as equivalent to role-specific support. Polish is served at `/`, English at `/en`; legacy `/pl` routes redirect to the canonical Polish surface.

Free output: overall score, detected/perceived profile, first-impression simulation, biggest issue and counts. Paid output: evidence-first issues, safe suggestions, ATS, strengths and ordered next steps. Job Match distinguishes missing skill from missing evidence and misplaced evidence.

CVUnfold helps candidates and is not an employer screening or ranking tool. Scores are document-quality indicators, never employment probability.

The analysis form has one career-stage choice: First job in tech, Junior, Mid, Senior, Lead or Auto. Bootcamp, university and self-directed learning share the First job stage. One stage-specific PL/EN prompt is selected before one normal model call; Auto infers and evaluates within that same call. Existing retry/fallback behavior applies only on eligible service failures. The first-job rubric uses beginner expectations without a commercial-experience score ceiling. Curated examples are fixed reports without a stage switch; the form links to the example for its selected stage. Legacy bootcamp URLs and context values normalize to First job. See `CAREER_AI_EVALUATION.md` for validation scope and the pending live-model quality gate.
