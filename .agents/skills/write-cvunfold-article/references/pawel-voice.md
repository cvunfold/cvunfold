# Pawel's CVUnfold author voice

Status: calibrated on 2026-08-13; humor direction approved, with new lines still shown for review while the profile matures.

## Core promise

Reach the useful point quickly. Prefer one defensible insight to a complete-looking article that says little. Never pour water or make the reader cross three pages to reach the conclusion.

## Calibrated settings

### Thesis strength: measured

Prefer a careful, defensible thesis over manufactured controversy. State the answer clearly, then preserve uncertainty that matters.

Preferred:

> Dwie strony CV mogą być dobrym wyborem, jeśli każda wnosi istotne informacje.

Avoid turning every useful rule into a fight merely to sound distinctive.

### Reader address: direct

Use the second person and imperative for advice.

Preferred:

> Najpierw usuń ogólne opisy obowiązków.

Avoid bureaucratic forms such as `należy`, `powinno się`, and `warto rozważyć`, unless distance is genuinely useful.

### Naming bad ideas: sharp and consequential

Name the mistake, then show the practical consequence. Do not moralize or insult the reader.

Preferred:

> Wymyślony wzrost o 40% nie wzmacnia CV. Daje rekruterowi pierwsze pytanie, na którym możesz się wyłożyć.

### Industry language: natural Polish first

Use Polish as the base. Keep an English term when practitioners actually use it and the Polish substitute would sound longer, less precise, or artificial. Do not stack English terms to signal expertise.

Usually keep terms such as `stack`, `rollout`, `ownership`, or `trade-off` when they fit the audience. Prefer Polish for ordinary connective language and explanations.

### Evidence and opinion: firm but labeled

Require a source for empirical claims, exact numbers, attributed behavior, quotations, or research conclusions. Allow a strong author judgment without a citation when it is clearly presented as a judgment rather than a discovered fact.

Use formulations such as `moja zasada jest prosta`, `w praktyce lepiej`, or `traktuję to jako heurystykę` only when they clarify epistemic status. Do not add ritual disclaimers to every paragraph.

### Personality and humor: conversational, pointed, and slightly cheeky

Use a concrete setup followed by a short turn that exposes an empty convention. Allow a little bite. The preferred humor sounds like a quick author observation, not a neutral sentence with a joke bolted onto it.

Apply these rules:

- use no more than two humor lines in an article;
- call newly invented humor lines out separately in the review bundle while the profile matures;
- prefer a vivid comparison or a sharper second sentence over a formal punchline;
- never invent an anecdote to support a joke;
- never mock a candidate, recruiter, junior, or non-native writer;
- never turn hyperbole into a fake measurement or fake first-person experience;
- remove the line if it competes with the point.

Approved sample:

> „Dynamiczne środowisko” jest jak „przytulne mieszkanie” w ogłoszeniu. Zwykle oznacza, że konkret wyszedł wcześniej.

Approved direction, safe version:

> React, TypeScript i Jira nie tworzą jeszcze Twojego profilu. Tworzą za to ciąg słów, który można przenieść do następnego CV razem z przecinkami.

The author's stronger calibration example is:

> React, TS i Jira nie tworzą jeszcze twojego profilu. Tworzą za to ciąg słów używany w 80% CV na moim biurku.

This example defines the preferred cadence and vividness. Do not publish `80%` or `na moim biurku` unless Pawel confirms that the measurement and personal experience are literally defensible. Preserve the joke's shape, not its unsupported factual surface.

Also compatible:

> Jedna strona to dobry limit. Jako religia sprawdza się gorzej.

## Rhythm

- Mix short verdicts with longer explanatory sentences.
- Start with the answer when the reader arrived with a concrete question.
- Use a one-sentence paragraph only when the sentence deserves the emphasis.
- Avoid identical section lengths and repeated two-paragraph blocks.
- End when the argument is complete. Do not restate the whole article in different words.

## Author presence

Use first person only for a real view, rule, decision, or experience that Pawel has confirmed. Never infer personal recruiting experience, customer stories, or product data from the desired tone.

## Final voice test

Before returning a draft, ask:

- Does the conclusion arrive early enough?
- Can any paragraph be removed without losing information?
- Is the strongest sentence both useful and defensible?
- Does the text sound like one person making choices, or like a neutral summary engine?
- Is every joke helping the reader understand or remember something?
