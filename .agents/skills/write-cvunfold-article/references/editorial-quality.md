# CVUnfold editorial quality

## Evidence classes

Classify material before the final edit:

- **Verified fact**: externally checkable and supported by a reliable source.
- **Project observation**: derived from approved, privacy-safe CVUnfold data. Never expose raw CV, report, prompt, filename, token, or PII content.
- **Author judgment**: a defensible interpretation or recommendation, stated as a view rather than disguised as research.
- **Illustration**: a constructed example clearly presented as an example, never as a customer or first-person story.

Narrow or remove a claim when its evidence class is unclear.

## Anti-water test

Keep a paragraph only when it contributes at least one of:

- a new claim;
- a concrete example;
- a consequence;
- a useful distinction;
- an action the reader can take;
- evidence that changes confidence in the argument.

Cut warm-up sentences, previews of obvious structure, repeated conclusions, and generic encouragement. Do not add a summary when the article is already short.

## Naturalness review

- Let structure follow the argument; do not enforce uniform section or paragraph counts.
- Vary rhythm because ideas need different space, not to manipulate a detector.
- Replace abstract chains with a person, action, object, constraint, or consequence.
- Search for repeated openings and constructions such as `nie chodzi o`, `nie tylko`, `warto`, `kluczowe jest`, and `podsumowując`.
- Remove sentences that merely announce importance.
- Do not add deliberate errors, slang, fragments, anecdotes, or rhetorical questions to simulate humanity.
- Treat fluent Polish and fluent English as separate editorial tasks.

## Humor review

Keep a humorous or ironic line only when all answers are yes:

1. Does it expose a real pattern or consequence?
2. Does it remain understandable without shared internet culture?
3. Is the target an empty convention or bad advice rather than a person?
4. Would the article be equally accurate without it?
5. Does it sound like an observation rather than a generated punchline?

If the fifth answer is uncertain, show the line as an option instead of inserting it silently.

## SEO and product fit

- Answer the actual query; do not repeat the exact phrase unnaturally.
- Keep title, description, slug, and H1 distinct in purpose while aligned in intent.
- Add an FAQ only for genuine follow-up questions not answered naturally in the article.
- Check existing PL and EN articles for cannibalization and repeated examples.
- Link only to pages that advance the reader's next question.
- Use the analysis CTA only when the article reveals a problem the product can honestly inspect.

## Review bundle checklist

- The thesis appears near the beginning.
- Facts, observations, judgments, and illustrations are distinguishable.
- Every paragraph adds information.
- No example pretends to be a real customer or author experience.
- Section lengths follow content rather than a template.
- English is adapted, not translated line by line.
- Reading time matches the actual text.
- Remaining uncertainty and source gaps are visible.
